### Crash Tag Team Racing (PS2, 2005)
- Port: `cttr-nx`, by Eladerino — static recompilation (ps2-native-recomp engine, via NyxBorn's PC port), rendered with deko3d
- Version: **initial public tree, 2026-11-18** — source-build only, no GitHub release yet; the build produces `cttr-nx.nro` from game files prepared on a PC from your own US disc (SLUS-21191)
- Status: early — boots and plays on real hardware per its README: legal screen, intro, menus and the park hub run, at about 10 FPS in the park hub at stock clocks; a full race has not been verified end to end yet
- Note: this repo returned 404 when checked in the 2026-11-17 evening run; it went public overnight
- Source: https://github.com/Eladerino/cttr-nx

### Tiny Toon Adventures: Buster's Hidden Treasure (Genesis / Mega Drive, 1993)
- Port: `BustersHiddenTreasure-NX`, by simplePsyduck — native port
- Version: **v1.0, released 2026-11-17** — the release ships `BustersHiddenTreasure_nx.nro`
- Status: released — widescreen (16:9 / 16:10), music player, level select, cheats and achievements per its release notes; own US ROM required
- Source: https://github.com/simplePsyduck/BustersHiddenTreasure-NX

### Dragon Ball Z: Budokai 3 HD (Xbox 360 HD Collection, 2012)
- Port: `Budokai3-nx`, by ramonDias07 — static recompilation (ReXGlue) of the Xbox 360 version, rendered with Deko3D, based on NovaPowers' PC recompilation
- Version: **v0.1.40, released 2026-11-17** — source-build: the release's tools generate `B3.nro` from your own USA/Japan Xbox 360 dump; announced in a GBAtemp release thread the same day
- Status: early — battles, campaign and menus tested on a physical Switch per its README; about 46 FPS average in the heaviest measured scenes, on test clocks of RAM 1600 MHz / CPU 1400 MHz / GPU 460 MHz (mildly overclocked)
- A separate earlier port of the same game (Budokai-NX, on GitLab) was referenced in the release thread but was not verified this run, so it is not listed
- Source: https://github.com/ramonDias07/Budokai3-nx
