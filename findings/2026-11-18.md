### Dragon Ball Z: Budokai 3 HD (Xbox 360 HD Collection, 2012)
- Port: `Budokai3-nx`, by ramonDias07 — static recompilation (ReXGlue) of the Xbox 360 version, rendered with Deko3D, based on NovaPowers' PC recompilation
- Version: **v0.1.40, released 2026-11-17** — source-build: the release's tools generate `B3.nro` from your own USA/Japan Xbox 360 dump; announced in a GBAtemp release thread the same day
- Status: early — battles, campaign and menus tested on a physical Switch per its README; about 46 FPS average in the heaviest measured scenes, on test clocks of RAM 1600 MHz / CPU 1400 MHz / GPU 460 MHz (mildly overclocked)
- A separate earlier port of the same game (Budokai-NX, on GitLab) was referenced in the release thread but was not verified this run, so it is not listed
- Source: https://github.com/ramonDias07/Budokai3-nx
