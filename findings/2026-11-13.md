# 2026-11-13 — baseline catalogue

First full catalogue of unofficial Nintendo Switch game ports, built from SwitchPorts (453 entries), GameBrew's List of Switch homebrew games and AllRecomps Switch (63 curated projects) — ~250+ distinct games itemised.

Highlights: GTA III (re3), GTA Vice City (reVC), GTA San Andreas / Liberty City Stories / Chinatown Wars (Android loaders), Max Payne 3 and Midnight Club 3 (ReXGlue recomps), Mario 64 / Ocarina of Time / Majora's Mask decomps, Doom / Quake / Half-Life / Morrowind / Daggerfall / Diablo / Fallout 1&2 source ports, and the 2026 Android-loader and static-recomp waves.

Standing caveats from day one:
- GTA IV is WIP with conflicting reports — do not call it playable/completable.
- GTA V / GTA5NX is cancelled — revealed Aug 2026, Take-Two contact 2026-09-28, project shut down 2026-10-03, code/assets never to be released, no legitimate download exists.
- Most Nov 2026 builds are v0.1–0.5, public but early/unrated for completability.
