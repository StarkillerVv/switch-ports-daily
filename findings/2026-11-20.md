Both absent from SwitchPorts, GameBrew and AllRecomps at scrub time; verified against their own repos.

### Transformers: War for Cybertron (Xbox 360, 2010)
- Port: `transformers-war-for-cybertron-nx`, by Aspirata — static recompilation (ReXGlue) of the Xbox 360 version, deko3d renderer; developed with AI assistance per the repo
- Version: **build-60, 20 Nov 2026** — source-build only (no prebuilt NRO; the build is produced locally from your own Xbox 360 copy with the ReXGlue SDK)
- Status: early — playable on Switch and close to the original per its README, with issues remaining: some shaders/effects missing (objects can disappear), occasional freezes, campaign not yet tested in full; ~28.5 FPS average in the author's build-55 session on an overclocked Switch (CPU 2397 MHz / GPU 768 MHz / RAM 2500 MHz), CPU-limited
- Source: https://github.com/Aspirata/transformers-war-for-cybertron-nx

### Super Stardust Delta (PS Vita, 2012)
- Port: `SuperStardustDeltaNX`, by DevCrashDay — static recompilation of the PS Vita game (PCSA00006, US v1.00) via the Nativ recompiler, deko3d runtime
- Version: **20 Nov 2026** — source-build only: the builder recompiles your own NoNpDrm dump locally and produces `SuperStardustDelta.nro` plus an installable forwarder NSP (Switch prod.keys required for the NSP)
- Status: source-build; brand-new repo (created 20 Nov 2026), no third-party testing at listing time
- Source: https://github.com/DevCrashDay/SuperStardustDeltaNX
