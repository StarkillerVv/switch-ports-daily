## New ports (5)

### F.E.A.R. (Xbox 360, 2005)
- Port: `fear-nx`, by infriker — static recompilation (ReXGlue) + Vulkan/Mesa NVK
- Version: v0.2.1, released 2026-11-06
- Status: menus + campaign playable, ~30 FPS at stock clocks (25 in heavy firefights), gyro aiming
- Needs your own Xbox 360 files (Title ID 565507D9)
- Source: https://github.com/infriker/fear-nx

### New Super Mario Bros. Wii (Wii, 2009)
- Port: `nsmbw-switch`, by schuler-ph — source-build static recomp (fork of WitherBlitz's NSMB Wii Multiplayer Recompilation)
- No prebuilt NRO; needs your own USA SMNE01 rev 1 disc
- Local co-op up to 4 players (2 measured); 59–60 FPS docked at 1785 MHz CPU, ~43 FPS stock
- AllRecomps reviewed 2026-11-14
- Source: https://github.com/schuler-ph/nsmbw-switch

### The Simpsons (arcade, Konami, 1991)
- Port: `simpsons-port`, by cheviman — decomp-based source port
- Version: v1.0.0 released 2026-11-14 (latest v1.0.1)
- Switch package with two .nro files (Arcade Game and Mr. Plow Edition) + NSP option; needs your own MAME `simpsons` 4P World set 1 ROM
- Source: https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port

### Madagascar (Xbox, 2005)

- Thread: https://gbatemp.net/threads/release-madagascar-xbox-for-nintendo-switch-native-static-recompilation-port-v0-1-8.686468/
