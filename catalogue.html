GTA V — GTA5NX (Paralympics Productions, lead Mojso / M0jso)

Native homebrew port · leak-based (alleged)Cancelled before wider release
What it was
A native port (not streaming/emulation/Linux/Android), reportedly based on GTA V Legacy v1.0.2699 (PC/PS4/XOne branch) and — per GamesRadar reporting — allegedly using leaked GTA V source code (an allegation, not confirmed by any published legal filing)
Performance
Revealed Aug 2026; Tom's Hardware, 2 Sep 2026, reported ~30fps with modifications; other testing: 10–25fps on an unmodified Switch, overclocking needed, aggressive asset compression for 4GB RAM, no GTA Online
Timeline
Rockstar mod guidelines of 10 Sep 2026 banned ports to platforms where games were not officially released; Discord server removed 23 Sep 2026 (copyright); Mojso announced 28 Sep 2026 that Take-Two lawyers had contacted the team; Paralympics Productions shut down 3 Oct 2026, cancelling the Switch build, its planned performance update and a parallel Android port

The team said it would not publish or transfer source code/assets. No legitimate download channel exists — any “GTA5NX download” site is not the developers. Paralympics Productions is verified only for GTA5NX plus that parallel (also cancelled) Android GTA V port — no other game by that team was found in any source.
