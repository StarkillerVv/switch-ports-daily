UNOFFICIAL / HOMEBREW PORTS ONLY · MODDED SWITCH (ATMOSPHÈRE / CFW) · YOU SUPPLY YOUR OWN GAME FILES

All games ported to Switch

Every game the research could confirm was unofficially ported to Nintendo Switch — not mods for one game, and not official eShop releases. That means homebrew .nro/NSP ports: decompilations, source ports, static recompilations and Android-binary loaders, from the classic 2018–2025 scene through the 2026 explosion that GTA5NX was part of.

Researched 15 November 2026 from three master catalogues — SwitchPorts (SP), GameBrew (GB) and AllRecomps (AR) — plus the projects' own GitHub repos and GBAtemp threads linked on the cards, and a direct GitHub scrub on 15 November 2026 for ports listed in none of the three catalogues (each repo checked live, with a real Switch build; marked “GitHub scrub find” below). Excluded as official eShop releases: GTA: The Trilogy – Definitive Edition (2021), Red Dead Redemption (Double Eleven, 2023), Portal: Companion Collection (2022), Sonic Mania, and the official Doom/Quake Bethesda ports. A few games below (e.g. the Sonic Mania decomp) exist unofficially despite also having an official version — noted where relevant.

Read “released” carefully for 2026 entries. In this report “released” means a public build/repo exists in SwitchPorts, GameBrew or AllRecomps — or was verified directly on GitHub in the 15 Nov 2026 scrub — as of 15 Nov 2026. SwitchPorts only introduced status icons (⬛ does not boot / 🟥 boots to title / 🟧 boots, unplayable / 🟨 playable / 🟩 completable / ✳️ feature-complete) in late 2026 and most entries are still unrated; many 2026 builds are v0.1–0.5 — bootable or playable-in-part rather than completable. Treat 2026 recomp/Android entries as released-but-early unless a performance note is given. Classic-era (pre-2026) entries are mostly mature and completable.

No confirmed port matches that search. Try the game's short name — or check the “cancelled / no port exists” section: some famous games were never ported at all.

08 Cancelled / rumoured / no port exists

The headline case is GTA V itself — the port this page is named after was cancelled, and there is no legitimate public download.

GTA V — GTA5NX (Paralympics Productions, lead Mojso / M0jso)

Native homebrew port · leak-based (alleged)Cancelled before wider release
What it was
A native port (not streaming/emulation/Linux/Android), reportedly based on GTA V Legacy v1.0.2699 (PC/PS4/XOne branch) and — per GamesRadar reporting — allegedly using leaked GTA V source code (an allegation, not confirmed by any published legal filing)
Performance
Revealed Aug 2026; Tom's Hardware, 2 Sep 2026, reported ~30fps with modifications; other testing: 10–25fps on an unmodified Switch, overclocking needed, aggressive asset compression for 4GB RAM, no GTA Online
Timeline
Rockstar mod guidelines of 10 Sep 2026 banned ports to platforms where games were not officially released; Discord server removed 23 Sep 2026 (copyright); Mojso announced 28 Sep 2026 that Take-Two lawyers had contacted the team; Paralympics Productions shut down 3 Oct 2026, cancelling the Switch build, its planned performance update and a parallel Android port

The team said it would not publish or transfer source code/assets. No legitimate download channel exists — any “GTA5NX download” site is not the developers. Paralympics Productions is verified only for GTA5NX plus that parallel (also cancelled) Android GTA V port — no other game by that team was found in any source. No other leak-source-based Switch port was verified either: the Android loaders (§04) load retail APK binaries at runtime, and the recomps (§05) are built from retail console discs.

09 What the research could not verify

Open questions, stated plainly — the report marks these as unverified rather than filling them in.

  • Playability/completability of most Nov 2026 (0.x) recomps and Android loaders — the catalogues list a build and a date, not a completion test. Only a handful have hard performance data: Dead Space 1 (540p30 stock / 720p30 OC), Tekken 3 (locked 60fps stock), SVR 2011 (~51fps OC), GTA V (10–25fps stock), GTA IV Theft4 build (0.4fps, conflicting with packaged GTA4NX videos).
  • Exact first-release dates for many classic ports — the GameBrew dates used here are its “last updated” for the Switch build, which may postdate first release (e.g. CorsixTH, SRB2).
  • Super Metroid (snesrev) and several snesrev-line ports — listed explicitly as WIP; no completable Switch build verified.
  • Switch 2 homebrew ports (HL2/Doom via ns2jb, Oct 2026) — video-verified only, no public release at the time of research; HL2 Switch 2 is in §07, not §01.
  • Dead projects: the SwitchPorts DEAD.md archive was not enumerated — cancelled pre-2026 projects beyond GTA5NX and the re3 litigation may exist there.
  • Minecraft Java 1.12.2 / Project Zomboid / Slay the Spire 2 — whether these catalogue entries are native ports vs. compatibility-layer recreations could not be confirmed from the catalogue alone; they appear in §04/§06 with that flag.

How to read this page: “Confirmed” means the game appears in one of the three master catalogues researched on 15 November 2026, or was verified directly in the GitHub scrub of the same date (repo live, real Switch build present) — SwitchPorts (453 entries, verified live), GameBrew's list of Switch homebrew games (author/version/date for classic-era ports), and AllRecomps (66 curated projects, checked Nov 2026) — which link each project's own GitHub/GBAtemp thread. Status wording is each source's own: where a catalogue says WIP, pre-release or unrated, this page says so too.

Scope: unofficial ports only. Every port here needs a modded Switch and your own legally-owned game files/ROM/APK — no game data is distributed with any of them. The previous version of this page catalogued only the mods and tools made for GTA5NX itself; this version covers all games ported to Switch, as requested.
